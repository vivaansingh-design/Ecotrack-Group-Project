Settings & calculator

PERSONALIZE
ELECTRICITY RATE
Use the per-unit rate from your electricity bill if known. Actual bills may include slabs, fixed charges and taxes.

Set your energy goal

Eco Score = 100 × (1 − estimated daily use ÷ target), limited to 0–100. Lower use relative to your chosen target gives a higher score. This is a transparent motivational indicator, not a scientific or official rating.

Quick calculator

Energy (kWh) = watts × hours ÷ 1,000. Cost = kWh × your saved tariff.

Data & privacy

Your appliance list, settings, daily snapshots and challenge status are stored in this browser on this device. They are not synced to a cloud account. Clearing browser data may erase them.
